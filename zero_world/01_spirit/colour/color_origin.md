# Color Origin

*zero_world/01_spirit/color_origin.md*

This isn't the zeroGravity brand story. That story is bigger, it belongs in the business repository, and it hasn't been written yet. This is narrower: why these specific colors, and why they were never really a choice.

It starts with a shirt. My dad passed it down to me, gold, black, and white, covered in symbols, letters, and numbers I used to think were just design. I always assumed it was Nigerian, since that's where I'm from, until someone stopped me and told me it was actually a Ghanaian pattern. It wasn't a coincidence that this palette found me either way. It's been present for most of my life without me ever asking why.

Now it's not just a shirt. I use it as the direct reference for Zero's own clothing, my digital twin, rendered inside my Operator Development build. The colors that were handed down to me are the same colors Zero wears.

Gold means the most to me personally. Among the Akan of Ghana, gold was considered incorruptible, a symbol of purity and immortality, because it doesn't corrode. That's exactly how I think about the work I've put in. It doesn't diminish. It's earned, and it stays earned.

The full research behind every color in the palette, what it means and where it comes from, lives in `10_research/color_meanings.md`. How those colors actually get used across the brand, website, clothing, animation, product design, lives in `09_design_language/color_system.md`. This file is just the root of it, why any of this started mattering to me in the first place.
