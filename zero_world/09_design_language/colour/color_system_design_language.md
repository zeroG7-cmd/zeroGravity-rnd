# Color System — Design Language

*zero_world/09_design_language/color_system.md*

How the researched palette (`10_research/color_meanings.md`) actually gets applied across zer0Gravity and Zero World. This file is the spec, not the "why."

## Locked (current)
**Black · Gold · Wine**
Black is the ground everywhere. Gold is Zero: grounded, earned, present, primary accent for headlines, CTAs, anything a client interacts with. Wine is the Zero Digital Twin: the fantasy/vision side, secondary accent, dividers, hover states.

## Roles, generally
- **Ground** — the darkest of whatever set is in use. Almost always black.
- **Primary accent** — the lightest, highest-contrast color against the ground. Carries headlines and calls to action.
- **Secondary accent** — the middle value. Dividers, borders, hover states, smaller UI moments.
- **Neutral** — beige, where body copy or breathing room is needed so the palette doesn't read as pure intensity everywhere.

## Where each context should pull from
- **Website** — locked palette (black/gold/wine) for the present-day business layer. Experimental combos (see below) reserved for deeper "vision" pages once tested.
- **Zero World clothing** — traced directly to the shirt passed down from your dad (see `01_spirit/color_origin.md`). Gold/black/white as the base reference, rendered on the digital twin.
- **Zero World animation** — draw from whichever combo matches the scene's register: void-anchored combos (black + gold + wine/purple/rust) for genesis or origin moments, non-void combos (navy/mauve/beige, wine/green/beige) for growth or in-progress moments.
- **Product & technical design** — Zero Command System UI, custom drone builds. Recommend locked palette for consistency with the business-facing brand, unless a specific product line wants its own identity.
- **Video color grading** — gold and wine as grade accents on delivered cinematography, black as the base grade, kept subtle rather than literal color overlays.
